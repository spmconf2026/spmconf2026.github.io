Registration Rules and Payment Guidelines
Base Registration Requirement: Every accepted paper must be covered by at least 1 Full registration.
Scope of the Full + Student Discount: The additional student discounts (two or more students) are exclusively applicable to a single accepted paper or to registrations without a paper.
Multiple Paper Policy: If a research group (e.g., an advisor and their students) has multiple accepted papers, an independent Full Registration is required for each paper.
Exception: If a single author is attending the conference alone and has no co-authors attending, their single Full Registration will cover all of their accepted papers.
Example Scenario: An author group of three people with two accepted/invited papers must purchase at least two Full Registrations to cover both papers. The third author can get a student ticket (if they are a student) or a full registration.
